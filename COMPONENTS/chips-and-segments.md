# Chips and segments

Chips choose modes, tools, tabs, and scenes without navigating away.

**Code:** Matrix `SurfaceChip` · system `Picker` `.segmented` where density requires · Site capability links (related pattern)

---

## SurfaceChip

Use for: Bridges/Devices/Apps, Settings tabs, sample rate/format/buffer, appearance System/White/Dark, Grid/List, Scenes A/B/C, Paint, speaker sets.

- Compact height 28, callout semibold
- Selected = primary pill treatment
- Optional marked dot (accent) or locked indicator
- Expose selected trait to VoiceOver

## Segmented pickers

System segmented controls are allowed when catalogs are tiny (Monitor | Matrix, I/O | Deep Room). Always `.quadraTint()`.

---

## Do

- Keep labels short (one word when possible).
- Group chips that form a single exclusive choice.

## Don’t

- Don’t replace a chip group with random custom tabs that ignore selected traits.
- Don’t use chips for destructive confirmation — use dialogs.
